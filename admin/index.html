Sign in

The instructor password signs every command, and each kiosk refuses anything it can't verify. The password never leaves this page. The GitHub side is handled by the club's API (brophy-fpv-club-api.netlify.app), which holds the repo token so this page doesn't have to.

Review Pilots Kiosks

Review

Photos pilots took of their work on the Pilot Path and Flight Clearance pages (TRUST certificates, signed Pilot Agreements), and Drone Portfolio entries - a picture, the drone's name and specs, and a Google Drive flight video (the card says whether the link is public and plays it). Recorded flights are Sim Flight checkrides from the kiosks: 60 seconds of Liftoff, played here next to the standard (Pass unlocks once the video has loaded). Pass files the same signed approval as the Approvals tab (both kiosks pull it) and tells the pilot; for a drone it approves that drone and files the Drone Portfolio badge. Fail tells the pilot why (a failed flight needs a note); they can send a new photo, or record another flight on a later day. The badge rules still apply: a checkpoint needs the rest of its tier.

Approve a badge

For bench and witnessed badges. The pilot has DM'd their photo (or you watched them do it); you approve here and a decision signed with your password is filed for that pilot. Both kiosks pull it on their next sync, whichever is on. Quiz badges are earned by passing the quiz — only use this to override. Nothing here can be undone by a student; Revoke is yours alone.

Approving a badge that is already earned is harmless.
