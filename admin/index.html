Sign in

The instructor password signs every command, and each kiosk refuses anything it can't verify. The password never leaves this page. The GitHub side is handled by the club's API (brophy-fpv-club-api.netlify.app), which holds the repo token so this page doesn't have to.

Review photos

Photos pilots took of their work on the Pilot Path and Flight Clearance pages (TRUST certificates, signed Pilot Agreements), and Drone Portfolio entries - a picture, the drone's name and specs, and a Google Drive flight video (the card says whether the link is public and plays it). Pass files the same signed approval as the Approvals tab (both kiosks pull it) and tells the pilot; for a drone it approves that drone and files the Drone Portfolio badge. Fail tells the pilot why, and they can send a new photo. The badge rules still apply: Sim Flight needs 5 sim hours, a checkpoint needs the rest of its tier.

Approve a badge

For bench and witnessed badges. The pilot has DM'd their photo (or you watched them do it); you approve here and a decision signed with your password is filed for that pilot. Both kiosks pull it on their next sync, whichever is on. Quiz badges are earned by passing the quiz — only use this to override. Nothing here can be undone by a student; Revoke is yours alone.

Approving a badge that is already earned is harmless.

Progress

Sources: kiosk = the kiosk’s last snapshot (data.json), approved = a decision on file from this console, quiz / lessons / steps = what the pilot did on the website. Approve and Revoke file the same signed decision as the form above (with its note); both kiosks apply it on their next sync, and a revoke clears the badge there even if the kiosk awarded it.

Sim Flight for everyone at 5+ hours

Pilots the kiosk shows with 5 or more sim hours who do not hold Sim Flight yet. One click files a signed approval for each (note: "5+ sim hours"); both kiosks apply them on their next sync. Revoke any one from their progress list.
