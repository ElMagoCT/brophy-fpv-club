Sim Flight
Log 5 sim hours, then fly a checkride in Liftoff.
Requirements
  1. 5 hours in the flight sims - Ground School time does not count
  2. Stick basics - four channels, rate vs angle, hover, throttle
  3. A clean orbit around a gate
  4. A Split-S
  5. Three gaps without a crash
  6. A finished race
In person with a mentor, everything in one flight, one attempt per day.
