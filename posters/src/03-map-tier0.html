  1. Stick basics · four channels, rate vs angle, hover, throttle

Build & techQuiz
Battery Care
  1. Pass the Battery Care quiz · 80 % or better · no sign-off needed
Covers: LiPo basics · Battery math · Charging, storage and disposal · When a pack goes bad
3
Build & techBench
Building
  1. Parts check: frame, FC/ESC, motors, RX, VTX, camera, antenna, props, strap
  2. Motors on - right screw length, nothing touching a winding
  3. Plug in the motors
  4. Assemble it and put the canopy on
  5. Plug in the canopy
  6. Test: motors beep, camera turns on
  7. Props: each matches its motor's spin (props in or out, as set in Betaflight), leading edge first
  8. Photo of your work on the website · a mentor passes it
4
Build & techBench
Betaflight Intro
  1. Props OFF, battery OUT, USB only
  2. Connect to Betaflight (the kiosk reads the board)
  3. Setup tab: calibrate the accelerometer, the drone flat and still
  4. Bind the receiver (plug/unplug method)
  5. Photo of your work on the website · a mentor passes it
Done on the club Meteor 75 Pro at the kiosk, right after Building. The rest of Betaflight is its own badge on the Tier 2 path.
