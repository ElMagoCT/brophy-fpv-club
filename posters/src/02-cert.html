02

Permission

every flight
1

Ask Mr. Burr

His Google Form, at least 24 hours before you want to fly. One flight, one request. A club meeting is one request for the whole group, filed by a mentor; a personal flight is your own.

2

Bring a spotter

Every flight has one, with eyes on the aircraft. Your own drone: anyone you name on the form. A club drone: a mentor is there for the session.

3

Wait for the yes

He answers by email. Fly once he approves, and only what you asked for. No answer means no.
