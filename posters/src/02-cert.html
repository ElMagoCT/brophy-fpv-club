Step 1 · once · never expires

Get Brophy Drone Certified

Four badges on the Flight Clearance page. About 45 minutes, at home, all four reviewed by a mentor.

  1. FAA TRUSTTake the free FAA TRUST test online (20 min) and send a photo of the certificate.
  2. Safe FlightPass the Safe Flight quiz: the Brophy rules, who flies, who watches, go / no-go.
  3. Pilot AgreementRead the terms, print the one-page agreement, sign it with a parent, send a photo.
  4. Drone PortfolioAdd the drone you will fly: a picture, its specs and a one-minute flight video.
Done? You are Brophy Drone Certified for every drone in your portfolio. Club drones are added when you pass their checkpoint on the Pilot Path.
Step 2 · every flight

Ask Mr. Burr, 24 hours ahead

His Google Form, once per flight. A club meeting is one request for the whole group, filed by a mentor; a personal flight is your own. Bring a spotter. He answers by email: fly only what he approved. No answer means no.
