2 · Permission

every flight
1
Ask Mr. Burr
His Google Form, at least 24 hours before you want to fly. One flight, one request. A club meeting is one request for the whole group, filed by a mentor; a personal flight is your own.
2
Spotter
Every flight has a spotter with eyes on the aircraft. Your own drone: anyone you name on the form. A club drone: a mentor is there for the session.
3
Wait for the yes
He answers by email. Fly once Mr. Burr approves, and only what you asked for. No answer means no.
