Build & techBench
Soldering
  1. Iron at the right temperature, tip tinned
  2. Tin the pad, tin the wire, join
  3. ESC pads: shiny, no bridges
  4. Motor wires: all three, trimmed
  5. Spin each motor in Betaflight (props off)
  6. Photo of your work on the website · a mentor passes it

Build & techBench
Betaflight
  1. Props OFF, battery OUT, USB only, then connect (the kiosk reads the board)
  2. Flash the practice config (a lightly messed-up drone)
  3. Receiver protocol; modes: ARM and angle; set the rates
  4. Motors: order and direction (props off!)
  5. OSD: battery usage and amp draw, positioned
  6. Check my work - the kiosk compares with the stock config
  7. Photo of your work on the website · a mentor passes it
