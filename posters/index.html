Printing

Paper
11×17 in (tabloid), portrait. Print at 100% / “actual size”, not “fit to page”. The PDFs have no margins; a printer that cannot bleed will add a thin white border, which is fine.
Order on the wall
Ad · Map 1 · 2 · 3 · 4 · Flight Clearance above the sim; the monitor strips cut in half and taped to the sides of each monitor. The four map sheets go edge to edge so the trail joins.
Changing them
Sources are in posters/src/ in the site repo. The map sheets and the Flight Clearance sheet come from gen_map.py, which reads every badge straight from flightschool/badges.js; the drone pictures are drawn by drones.py, the docks by gen_dock.py; build.py renders everything to PDF and PNG with headless Chrome.
Design matches the kiosk: paper, grid, ink. Screenshots on the how-to come from the kiosk itself. mtucker27@ for questions
