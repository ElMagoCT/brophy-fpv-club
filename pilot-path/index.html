How it works

video + quiz · hands-on · flying · checkpoints
Video + quiz
Most badges start with a short club video and a quiz. 80 % passes and your best score counts. A quiz badge is yours as soon as you pass.
Hands-on
Building, Soldering, Betaflight and Tuning: follow the Instructions, then submit a photo and a mentor checks it.
Flying
Video and quiz first, then a mentor watches you fly it. Sim Flight is recorded on a sim instead.
Checkpoints
Each tier ends in a checkpoint flight. It opens once every badge before it is earned. Event Pilot works the same way. Passing isn’t permission to fly: get Flight Clearance, then ask Mr. Burr 24 h before every campus flight.
Already fly?
If you’ve already proven a skill, a mentor can check that badge off. Show them at the club.

The badges

Sim hours and earned badges come from the club kiosk. Quizzes, lessons and ticks you do here are saved for the pilot you picked and synced to the club (they only ever go up, and the kiosk has the final say). Mentors approve badges in the console. mtucker27@ for questions
