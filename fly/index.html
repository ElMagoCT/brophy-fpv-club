The rule
No badge or clearance is permission to fly. Before every flight on campus, send the request at least 24 hours ahead. Mr. Burr approves or denies it; you fly only after a yes, as requested.
1 · Get cleared
Finish the four parts of Flight Clearance: FAA TRUST, Safe Flight, the Pilot Agreement, a drone in your portfolio.
2 · Ask
Fill in the form at least 24 hours before you want to fly. One flight, one request: that drone, that place, that time.
3 · Spotter
Every flight has a spotter. If you are not a Mentor yet, your spotter must be one.
4 · Wait for the yes
Fly only after Mr. Burr approves, and only as requested. No answer is a no.
