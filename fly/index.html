The rule
Passing a checkpoint never grants permission to fly. Before every flight on campus, send this request at least 24 hours ahead. Mr. Burr approves or denies it; you fly only after a yes, as requested.
1 · Ask
Fill in the form below at least 24 hours before you want to fly. One flight, one request.
2 · Spotter
Every flight has a spotter. If you are not a Mentor yet, your spotter must be one.
3 · Wait for the yes
Fly only after Mr. Burr approves, and only as requested: that pilot, that aircraft, that place, that time.
