Privacy

Brophy FPV Club website · what we keep
Home

The club keeps as little as it can: a name, your progress, and your sim hours. Nothing is sold, shared for advertising, or used for anything but running the club.

What we keep

  • Your name as you add it on the Join page: first name and last initial.
  • Your progress: quiz scores, badges, checkpoint results, mentor notes, and the photo or Drive links you submit.
  • Sim hours logged on the club simulators.
  • Your school email address, if a mentor added it, to send you sign-in codes.

Sign in with Google

  • Sign-in is only for your Brophy school account, so nobody can post progress under someone else's name.
  • Google tells us your name, your school email address and an account number. We use the name to fill in the Join form. We keep the part of your email before the @ and the account number, only to link your sign-in to your club entry.
  • A mentor can add your school email address to your club entry. When you choose "Email me a code", we send a 6-digit sign-in code there, and only then. The address is never shown on the site or shared.
  • We never read your email, Drive, contacts or anything else in your Google account, and we never post anything for you.

Who can see it

  • Names and earned badges show on the club site and the sims. Everything else is seen only by club mentors.
  • The data is stored on GitHub and Netlify, the services that run the site.

Removing it

  • Ask a mentor or the club moderator, and your entry and sign-in link are removed.
