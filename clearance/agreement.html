Pilot Agreement

Brophy FPV Club · Flight Clearance · signing paper
pilot + parent

You are about to fly expensive equipment on school property. This paper is a plain reminder that you are responsible for how you use it. It is kept simple on purpose. The full terms are on the club website under Flight Clearance → Pilot Agreement; this is the short version you sign.

The short version

  • I am responsible for any loss or damage to club equipment, property or people that results from my flying. If I break something, I replace it within 14 days - the same model or a mentor-approved equivalent - by buying it, paying the club, or working it off through Brophy's BEAST program on a plan agreed with club leadership. My flight privileges pause until that is done.
  • Malfunctions and outside events (a gust, a bird, someone grabbing the drone) can release me - with evidence such as my goggle DVR, which I record and keep for every flight. Failed tricks, flying out of range, past low-battery warnings, in bad or windy conditions, or with gear I knew was damaged are mine.
  • I fly club equipment only with a mentor and a spotter present, in approved areas at approved times. Flying without them makes me fully responsible, no exceptions. A mentor may end any flight.
  • I inspect the drone before every flight and report every crash or incident to a mentor at once, even if nothing looks broken.
  • My own drone is entirely my responsibility - its damage, its registration, and anything it causes.
  • Clearance lets me ask to fly; it is not permission, not a guarantee of my skill, and the club can suspend it. My parent or guardian and I release the club, its mentors, its moderator and Brophy College Preparatory from claims arising from my flying, and we accept that FPV flying carries real risks - propellers, batteries, collisions.

Tick each box - you are saying it is true

I know the local FAA rules. I hold a TRUST certificate, I keep the drone in sight with a spotter, I never fly over people, and I respect airspace and altitude limits.
I know the Brophy flight guidelines. I passed Safe Flight, I know the rules sheet by the simulators, and I follow it every time.
I will submit the flight form before every flight. Mr. Burr's form on the Flight Clearance page, at least 24 hours ahead, and I fly only after his yes - as requested.
I am liable for any and all damages to club equipment, property or people that come from my flying, as the terms describe.
